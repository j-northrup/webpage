My research focuses on grey wolf ecology in North Western Ontario. With ongoing moose population declines, my goal is to investigate predator-prey dynamics by analyzing wolf diet composition and its impact on moose. This research will use diet analysis methods alongside GPS data from collared wolves, collected as part of a broader multi-species monitoring project. Findings will inform Crown Land management, forest harvest planning, and wildlife conservation strategies across North Western Ontario.
